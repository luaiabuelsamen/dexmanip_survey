% Figure: why a retargeted pose can be scored good and still be inside the object. Drawn for the
% LaTeX edition in the restrained palette: greyscale plus the one accent, thin rules, square
% corners, document font. It replaces a reproduced photograph (one cell of a retargeting-artifact
% grid, a fingertip driven through the temple of a pair of spectacles), which showed that the
% failure exists but not why the metric misses it. The mechanism is geometric, so it is drawn:
% the error the metric reports is a sum over joints, and the failure is a distance at one surface.
%
% Left, the metric's view: three joint angles, each a few degrees off the human reference, each
% inside whatever per-joint tolerance is set. Right, the same pose at the contact: those few
% degrees compose along the finger into a fingertip displacement, and a thin object is thinner
% than that displacement, so the fingertip is through it.
%
% The figure prints no measurement. Every quantity behind it is stated with its citation in
% Sec. V: TopoRetarget constrains penetration during retargeting with a 1 mm soft tolerance and a
% 30 mm hard bound and reports maximum depth and the fraction of frames past 2 mm
% (toporetarget_2026); it is the only corpus method that treats penetration as an evaluation
% quantity, and it measures it on the reference rather than on the rollout. The stated retargeting
% objectives that do not carry an object term at all are Sec. V's list, from a fingertip
% keypoint-vector energy (anyteleop_2023) to a per-joint regression (dexumi_2025).
%
% Geometry: drawn 8.2 cm wide so that at one column the \scriptsize labels stay near 7 pt. Left
% panel x 0-3.6, right panel x 4.2-8.2, a hairline between them, the note under a bottom rule.
\begin{tikzpicture}[
  font=\footnotesize,
  palm/.style={draw=black!45,fill=wash,line width=0.3pt},
  ref/.style={draw=black!50,line width=0.5pt,dash pattern=on 1.6pt off 1.4pt},
  reftip/.style={draw=black!50,line width=0.5pt,dash pattern=on 1.6pt off 1.4pt,fill=none},
  rob/.style={draw=accent,line width=0.7pt},
  robtip/.style={draw=accent,line width=0.7pt,fill=white},
  obj/.style={draw=black!65,line width=0.5pt,fill=black!12},
  over/.style={draw=none,fill=accent,fill opacity=0.22},
  dim/.style={draw=accent,line width=0.5pt,<->},
  lead/.style={draw=black!45,line width=0.28pt},
  ax/.style={draw=black!55,line width=0.4pt},
  tol/.style={draw=black!45,line width=0.35pt,dash pattern=on 1.4pt off 1.2pt},
  bar/.style={draw=black!45,fill=black!22,line width=0.3pt},
  hd/.style={font=\scriptsize\bfseries,anchor=north,align=center},
  sub/.style={font=\scriptsize\color{black!60},anchor=north,align=center,text width=34mm},
  glab/.style={font=\scriptsize\color{black!60},anchor=west},
  alab/.style={font=\scriptsize\color{accent},anchor=west},
  tk/.style={font=\scriptsize\color{black!60},anchor=north},
  note/.style={font=\scriptsize,anchor=north west,align=left},
]

\node[hd] at (1.80,3.12) {what the joint-angle error sees};

% the human reference the retargeting is fitted to
\draw[ref] (0.45,2.45) -- ++(-4:0.70) coordinate (t1)
                       -- ++(-22:0.55) coordinate (t2)
                       -- ++(-46:0.42) coordinate (t3);
\draw[reftip] (t3) circle (0.15);
% the retargeted pose: every joint a few degrees off, and the errors compose
\draw[rob]    (0.45,2.45) -- ++(-10:0.70) coordinate (r1)
                          -- ++(-31:0.55) coordinate (r2)
                          -- ++(-58:0.42) coordinate (r3);
\draw[robtip] (r3) circle (0.15);
\fill[accent] (0.45,2.45) circle (0.05);

% ==================================================================== the point

\node[note,text width=79mm] at (0,-0.50)
  {\textcolor{accent}{\textbf{The gap.}} Joint-angle error is a sum over joints and carries no term
   for the object, so it cannot see the right-hand panel at all. Errors small enough to pass at
   every joint compose along the finger into a fingertip displacement larger than a thin object is
   thick, and the pose is scored as good with the object inside the finger. Sec.~V's one method
   that measures penetration measures it on the reference, before the policy runs.};
\end{tikzpicture}

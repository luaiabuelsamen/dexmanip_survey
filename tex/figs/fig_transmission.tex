% Figure: where the motor sits, and what each answer costs. Drawn for the LaTeX edition in the
% restrained palette: greyscale plus the one accent, thin rules, square corners, document font.
% It replaces four reproduced photographs (a direct-drive hand, an exposed linkage transmission,
% a coupled four-bar phalanx and a screenshot of tendons routed over wrapping cylinders) with the
% mechanism they were standing in for, which none of them actually drew: what the drive does to
% the fingertip's reachable set, and what it costs to know where the joint is.
%
% Every quantity the rows allude to is stated with its citation in Sec. III: LEAP's 19.5 N
% pull-out against the Allegro's 8.5 N at 595 g (leap_hand_2023); ILDA's 15 motors in the palm,
% 1.1 kg and 34 N fingertip (ilda_hand_2021); Shadow's 4.3 kg with the forearm
% (shadow_dexterous_hand_2005); BiDexHand's four-bar driving each DIP off its PIP
% (bidexhand_2025); the Faive Hand's EKF joint angles failing under contact (faive_hand_2023) and
% Ruka-v2's 8.26 degrees over seven joints (ruka_v2_2026). The figure prints no number itself.
%
% The right-hand column is the load-bearing part. Under an independent drive the fingertip
% reaches a region of the plane; under a coupling the joints are tied, so it reaches a curve
% through that region and nothing off it. That is what an actuator saved actually costs, and it
% is the one thing a photograph of a linkage cannot show.
%
% Geometry: three rows on a 1.90 cm pitch, drawing at x 0-2.6, text at 3.0-6.3, reachable set at
% 6.5-8.2. Drawn at 8.2 cm so that at one column the \scriptsize labels stay near 7 pt.

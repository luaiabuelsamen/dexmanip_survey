% ---- where penetration comes from -----------------------------------------
\node[pnl,anchor=north west,text width=17.2cm] (notes) at (0.4,-3.15) {%
  \scriptsize
  \textbf{\textcolor{accent}{Where penetration comes from: three sources on different knobs.
  Which one dominates in a hand has not been measured.}}\par\vspace{2pt}
  \textbf{Velocity-level enforcement.} Where non-penetration is enforced on velocities, any
  residual approach velocity is integrated into overlap of order $v \cdot \Delta t$. Illustration
  under an assumption, not a measurement: a fingertip closing at 0.5 m/s, a speed no source in this
  survey reports, at Isaac Gym's 1/120 s Shadow Hand step gives about 4 mm before the next step's
  constraint acts. Engines that enforce the gap at the next configuration carry no such term:
  Dojo's hard-contact NCP holds its feet above the floor at $\Delta t = 0.1$, $0.01$ and $0.001$ s
  alike.\par\vspace{1.5pt}
  \textbf{The prescribed compliance.} A regularised contact rests at a violation equal to the
  normal load times the compliance that was chosen for it. It is zero at zero load, and MuJoCo's
  closed form is independent of the object's mass. The depth is a setting, and no engine paper in
  \tablename~\ref{tab:simulators} other than ComFree-Sim states the setting it
  ships.\par\vspace{1.5pt}
  \textbf{Solver truncation.} A truncated solve degrades with the conditioning of the problem and
  with redundancy in the contact set, not with stiffness, which is prescribed. A grasp guarantees
  both, being many persistent contacts on a light object.};

\node[pnl,anchor=north west,text width=17.2cm] (geo) at (0.4,-6.33) {%
  \scriptsize
  Not a source of overlap, but a reason a number is ambiguous: an outer convex hull or
  decomposition contains the visual mesh, so it blocks contacts and hides overlap measured against
  that mesh, while an inscribed primitive admits overlap the solver never sees. PhysX~5 uses SDF
  collision on non-convex bodies and skips the decomposition. A penetration number must name the
  geometry it used.\par\vspace{2pt}
  \textbf{The depth is computable from the poses and the meshes. IsaacGymEnvs' IndustReal task
  already gates its policy update on it at 1 mm.}};

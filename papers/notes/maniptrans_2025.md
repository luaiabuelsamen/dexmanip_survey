## One-line contribution
Two-stage transfer of human hand-object MoCap to simulated dexterous hands: a frozen, generalist hand-trajectory imitator (RL, hand-only data) plus a per-task residual policy trained with object-following and contact-force rewards under a gravity/friction/threshold curriculum; used to build DexManipNet (3.3K episodes, 61 tasks) and replayed on real Inspire hands (abstract; Sec. 1).

## Setting
- hand(s): Inspire Hand, simulated as 12-DoF ("enhancing flexibility compared to its real-world 6-DoF mechanism", Sec. 3.4); also Shadow (22), articulated MANO (22), Allegro (16) (Sec. 4.4); code adds XHand and Inspire FTP (README). No arm in sim: the wrist is a free 6-DoF body driven by force/torque (Sec. 3.1). bimanual (two hands, two objects).
- simulator / physics: Isaac Gym, 4096 envs, dt 1/60 s, RTX 4090 (Sec. 4.2). Code main/cfg/task/ResDexHand.yaml: `dt: 0.0166667`, `substeps: 2`, PhysX `num_position_iterations: 8`, `contact_offset: 0.005`, `rest_offset: 0.0`, `max_depenetration_velocity: 1000.0`, `contact_collection: 1` (last substep); yaml default `numEnvs: 8192`, README commands use `num_envs=4096`; `episodeLength: 1200` (imitator yaml: 2000).
- observation (paper): imitator s_I = {target hand trajectory τ_h, proprioception q_d, q̇_d, w_d, ẇ_d}; residual adds s_interact = {τ_o, object pos relative to wrist, velocities, centre of mass, gravity vector, BPS shape encoding, squared keypoint-object distances D, contact force C} (Sec. 3.2-3.3). Code ResDexHand.yaml `obsKeys: q, cos_q, sin_q, base_state` (base position zeroed: "# ! ignore base position", dexhandmanip_bih.py `compute_observations_side`); `privilegedObsKeys: dq, manip_obj_pos, manip_obj_quat, manip_obj_vel, manip_obj_ang_vel, tip_force, manip_obj_com, manip_obj_weight`, dim `ndof+13+5*4+3+1` per hand. BPS is not among the yaml keys.
- action space: per hand, joint PD targets a_q ∈ R^K plus 6-DoF wrist force a_w ∈ R^6; final action a = a_I + Δa_R, clipped to joint limits (Sec. 3.1, 3.3). Code: `actionScale: 1.0`, `translationScale: 1.0`, `orientationScale: 0.1`, `actionsMovingAverage=0.4` in README commands. 60 fps.
- objects / data: quantitative eval on OakInk-V2 validation, ~80 episodes of 4-20 s at 60 fps, "approximately half ... bimanual" (Sec. 4.1). Imitator pre-trained on hand-only datasets [14, 36, 62, 107, 134, 137, 144] plus interpolated synthetic data, mirrored for left/right, ~1.5 days on one GPU (Sec. 3.2; App. C). DexManipNet from FAVOR and OakInk-V2: 61 tasks, 3.3K episodes, 1.2K objects, 1.34M frames, ~600 bimanual sequences (Sec. 3.4; App. E). GRAB and ARCTIC used qualitatively (Sec. 4.1; App. A.1, Table 3).

## Method
- paradigm: RL (two-stage, residual) ; algorithm: PPO for both stages, horizon 32, minibatch 1024, γ 0.99, Adam lr 5e-4 with decay (Sec. 4.2). README commands use `learning_rate=2e-4`, `early_stop_epochs=1000` for bimanual.
- coordination of the two hands: the paper formulates d = {d_l, d_r} acting on o = {o_l, o_r}, one object per hand, "in a cooperative manner (e.g., ... one hand holds the cap while the other grips the pen body)" (Sec. 3.1). Code (`side=BiH`, maniptrans_envs/lib/envs/tasks/dexhandmanip_bih.py): two frozen imitators (`rh_base_model_checkpoint`, `lh_base_model_checkpoint`), one residual policy whose observation is the concatenation of the right- and left-side observation dicts (`compute_observations`: `torch.cat([obs_rh[k], obs_lh[k]])`), so each hand sees the other's proprioception and object state through the shared vector; there is no explicit relative-hand feature. Reward is the sum of per-side rewards (`self.rew_buf = rh_rew_buf + lh_rew_buf`), reset if either side fails (`rh_reset_buf | lh_reset_buf`), success only if both succeed (`rh_success_buf & lh_success_buf`). Roles are fixed by the demonstration (each side tracks its own MoCap hand and object).
- reward (paper): imitator r_I = w_wrist r_wrist + w_finger r_finger + w_smooth r_smooth, with r_wrist on the SE(3) wrist pose and velocity error, r_finger a keypoint-matching sum over F = 21 keypoints with per-keypoint weights w_f and decay rates λ_f (Eq. 1, recovered as the raw LaTeX `r^t_{\ t ex t { fi n ger }} = \t ex t st yl e  \sum _{f=1}^F { w_{f} \cdot \exp {(-\lambda _{f} \|\mbold {j}_{{\dexhand _f}}^t - \mbold {j}_{{\hand _f}}^t\|\smash {_{\scriptscriptstyle 2}^{\scriptscriptstyle 2}})}} \label {eq:finger_reward} `, i.e. r^t_finger = Σ_{f=1}^F w_f · exp(−λ_f ‖j^t_{dexhand_f} − j^t_{hand_f}‖²₂) once the injected spaces are read out of the command names (recovered by OCR from papers/md/maniptrans_2025.ocr.md; OCR text is noisier than the layout parse, symbols may be imperfect)), r_smooth penalising joint power (velocity × torque) (Sec. 3.2). Residual r_R = r_I + w_object r_object + w_contact r_contact; r_object on object pose and velocity error; r_contact "Encourages appropriate contact force when the hand-object distance in the MoCap dataset is below a specified threshold ξ_c" via an indicator on fingertip-surface distance times contact force (Eq. 2, recovered as the raw LaTeX `r^t_{\t e xt {co n tac t} } = w _{ \ t e xt { c} } \ c d ot \e xp {(\frac {-\lambda _{\text {c}}}{\textstyle \sum _{f=1}^F\mbold {C}_{\dexhand _f}^t \cdot \mathds {1}\left (\mbold {D}(\mbold {j}_{\hand _f}^t, \mbold {p}_{\obj }^t \cdot \obj ) < \xi _{\text {c}}\right )})} \label {eq:contact_reward} `, i.e. r^t_contact = w_c · exp(−λ_c / Σ_{f=1}^F C^t_{dexhand_f} · 1(D(j^t_{hand_f}, p^t_obj · obj) < ξ_c)) — note the decay is over a *reciprocal*: −λ_c divided by the summed contact force, so the reward rises as contact force accumulates at fingertips that are within ξ_c of the object surface (recovered by OCR from papers/md/maniptrans_2025.ocr.md; OCR text is noisier than the layout parse, symbols may be imperfect)). The total residual-stage reward is given in the same paragraph as `r^t_R = r^t_I + w_object · r^t_object + w_contact · r^t_contact` (Sec. 3.3). App. D.2, Table 5 (w_f, λ_f per proximal/intermediate/distal/tip): thumb 0.5,0.3,0.3,0.9 / 50,40,40,100; index 0.5,0.3,0.3,0.8 / 50,40,40,90; middle 0.5,0.3,0.3,0.75 / 50,40,40,80; ring and pinky 0.5,0.3,0.3,0.6 / 50,40,40,60; missing joints get zero. Contact: "we set both parameters, w_c and λ_c, to 1". Articulated objects add |θ − θ̂| and |θ̇ − θ̂̇| terms (App. A.1).
- reward (code, dexhandmanip_bih.py `compute_imitation_reward`, visible part): `reward_eef_pos = exp(-40·‖Δp_wrist‖)`, `reward_thumb_tip_pos = exp(-100·d)`, index `exp(-90·d)`, middle `exp(-80·d)`, pinky and ring `exp(-60·d)`, `reward_level_1_pos = exp(-50·d)`, `reward_level_2_pos = exp(-40·d)`, `reward_eef_vel`, `reward_eef_ang_vel`, `reward_joints_vel` all `exp(-1·mean|Δv|)`, `reward_eef_rot = exp(-1·|angle|)`, `reward_obj_pos = exp(-80·‖Δp_obj‖)`; in dexhandmanip_sh.py additionally `reward_power = exp(-10·power)`, `reward_wrist_power = exp(-2·wrist_power)` where power = Σ|τ·q̇| (`compute_reward_side`). The decay rates match Table 5's λ_f (tip 100/90/80/60/60; proximal 50; intermediate/distal 40); the w_f weights, the object-rotation term, the contact term and the weighted sum are past the truncation and cannot be checked. `tip_force` is read from `net_cf` per contact body and a `tips_contact_history` mask is built (dexhandmanip_bih.py `compute_reward_side`).
- curriculum (paper Sec. 3.3, 4.2): ε_finger 6 cm → 4 cm (8 cm for Allegro, App. A.2); ε_object 90° / 6 cm → 30° / 2 cm; gravity starts at 0 and is restored; friction starts high and is reduced; RSI from "a non-colliding near-object state"; early termination on object deviation and on missing contact force when the human grasp distance is below ξ_t. Code: `tightenMethod: exp_decay`, `tightenFactor: 0.7`, `tightenSteps: 3200`; failure test `diff_thumb_tip_pos_dist > 0.04 / 0.7 * scale_factor` (dexhandmanip_sh.py); randomisation gravity `scaling ... linear_decay, schedule_steps 1920, init_value 0`; object friction `init_value 3, upper 6.0, lower 1.0, linear_decay 1920` (ResDexHand.yaml `task.randomization_params`). Mismatch: paper says thresholds are "gradually decreasing"; yaml uses exp decay over 3200 frames.
- interpenetration / contact: objects replaced by convex hulls (Sec. 3.3) and made watertight with ManifoldPlus, sizes "slightly adjust[ed] ... to enhance object-object interactions (e.g., the cap and body of the bottle)" (App. E); density 200 kg/m³ unless measured (App. D.3). App. D.3: kinematics-based grasp methods "often permit slight penetration between fingertips and object surfaces"; ManipTrans instead sets friction "slightly higher than the real-world value" to compensate for missing skin deformation. Contact force is observation, reward and termination condition (Sec. 4.6). PhysX `max_depenetration_velocity: 1000.0`. No penetration metric.
- key trick(s): residual initialised zero-mean with a warm-up (Sec. 3.3); ~15 min residual training vs QuasiSim ~40 h for a 60-frame clip (Sec. 4.3); rollouts low-pass filtered with α = 0.4 for the dataset (App. E).

## Evaluation
- metrics (Sec. 4.1): E_r per-frame object rotation error (deg), E_t translation error (cm), E_j mean per-joint position error (cm), E_ft mean per-fingertip error (cm, M = 5 single / 10 bimanual); SR = all four below 30°, 3 cm, 8 cm, 6 cm; "For bimanual tasks, the trajectory is considered failed if either hand fails". Errors are computed "only on successfully rolled-out sequences" (Table 1 caption).
- headline numbers (Table 1, OakInk-V2 val, Inspire): ManipTrans E_r 8.60, E_t 0.49, E_j 2.15, E_ft 1.36, SR 58.1 / 39.5 (single / bimanual); Retarget+Residual 11.58 / 0.79 / 2.54 / 1.74 / 47.8 / 13.9; RL-Only 9.72 / 1.23 / 2.96 / 2.38 / 34.3 / 12.1; Retarget-Only SR 4.6 / 0.0. Noise robustness (Table 4, single-hand): σ = 1.5 cm gives SR 46.7 (the "/ 39.2" second column is unexplained for a single-hand test; ambiguous). Policy learning on DexManipNet bottle rearrangement (Table 2; 140 sequences, 85/15 split, 20 rollouts per sequence, success = within 10 cm): IBC 4.69 %, BET 9.69 %, DP-UNet 18.44 %, DP-Transformer 14.69 %.
- baselines beaten: RL-Only, Retarget+Residual, Retarget-Only (all re-implemented; Table 1); QuasiSim qualitatively only (Fig. 4). Ablations are curves only (Fig. 7): contact force as reward raises SR, as observation speeds convergence; without threshold relaxation "the network may fail to converge entirely" (Sec. 4.6).
- real robot? yes, replay only — two 7-DoF Realman arms, Inspire Hands with tactile sensors; sim 12-DoF joints fitted to the real 6-DoF by fingertip alignment plus temporal smoothness, arm IK to the wrist, "we do not enforce strict temporal alignment" (Sec. 4.5). No trial counts or success rates; Fig. 6 qualitative.

## Limitations stated by the authors (Sec. 5)
- "some sequences cannot be transferred effectively ... 1) excessive noise in interaction poses and 2) insufficiently accurate object models for simulation, particularly for articulated objects."
- Real deployment is open-loop replay, not a learned real-world policy (Sec. 4.5).
- "Accurately simulating contact-rich scenarios remains an area for future exploration." (App. D.3)

## Quotable claims (verbatim, with section)
- "Our key insight is to treat the transfer as a two-stage process: a pre-training trajectory imitation stage focusing on hand motion alone, followed by a specific action fine-tuning stage that meets interaction constraints." (Sec. 1)
- "the Retargeting method often causes collisions in contact-rich scenarios, resulting in instability during residual policy training." (Sec. 4.3)
- "to compensate for the absence of skin deformation in simulation, we set the friction coefficient F slightly higher than the real-world value." (App. D.3)
- "For bimanual tasks, the trajectory is considered failed if either hand fails to meet these conditions" (Sec. 4.1)

## Notes for the survey
- Feeds: bimanual tracking from MoCap; residual-on-imitator architecture; contact force as obs/reward/termination; curriculum by physics relaxation; DexManipNet as a data source.
- Bimanual here means two hands on two objects (pen + cap, bottle + lid) with independent per-hand rewards summed; ARCTIC single-object bimanual is App. A.1 qualitative only. Contrast dexmachina_2025 (one articulated object, joint task reward) and its finding that this curriculum does not help there.
- Penetration is neither measured nor penalised; the stated stance is to raise friction rather than allow penetration. The bimanual SR (39.5 %) vs single (58.1 %) gap is the quotable bimanual penalty.
- The paper's PPO lr (5e-4) and env count (4096) differ from the shipped yaml/README (2e-4, 8192 default); cite the paper values as the paper's and the README as the reproduction recipe.

## Description

The XHAND1 offers a highly capable, fully actuated robotic hand that supports natural motion and complex manipulation. It moves with 12 active degrees of freedom, letting each finger operate independently. As a result, the hand can perform precise and varied grasping tasks with confidence. Because Robotera equips it with high-resolution tactile sensors on every fingertip, the hand senses contact, force, and even temperature during interaction. This tactile feedback helps robots adapt their grip in real time, which improves handling of both delicate and heavy objects.

## **Simulation ready.**

The Ability Hand’s [Open API](https://github.com/psyonicinc/ability-hand-api) enables full system integration: speed, multi-touch capabilities, and toughness make it a favorite among roboticists and researchers.

Torque, velocity, and position control of the 6 brushless DC motors as well as streaming of all 6 encoder values and 30 touch sensor values over BLE, I2C, UART, or RS485. Our [_datasheet_](https://github.com/psyonicinc/ability-hand-api/blob/master/Documentation/ABILITY-HAND-ICD.pdf) contains electrical specs, dimensions for our wrist connection, and commands you can send to the hand. **Our**[** _spec sheet_**](https://www.psyonic.io/s/Robotics-Spec-Sheet_06-000006.pdf)**contains our product information and technical specifications.**

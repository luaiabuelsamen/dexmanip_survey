# RH56DFX

Leading cumulative shipments, proven stable performance, adaptable to diverse scenarios.

The DFX series humanoid five finger dexterous hand is a perfect combination of strength and speed,with a thumb active output force of 1.5KG and a four finger fingertip output force of 1KG. Integratedabsolute position sensors and force sensors, it provides real-time force feedback. Power off self-locking, no need to change position when powering on.

# The Next Stage of Robotics is Manipulation

Sharpa Wave is a human-scale, five-finger dexterous hand with 22 active DoF, high-resolution tactile sensing for physical AI research and robot integration.

[ Enquire about Wave ](/pages/contact) View Specifications [ Full Video ](https://youtu.be/GcTUlOHvdOs?si=DtEVBltXdkqI4SCf)

1:1 Scale

Human Form

Understand Every Contact 

The palm width to hand length ratio is approximately 0.618, allowing the Wave to manipulate the same range of tools as humans. 

22 Active

Degrees of Freedom

Sharpa Wave's unique isomorphic design mirrors the human hand, unlocking limitless real-world tasks with human-level dexterity. 

Groundbreaking Dynamic Tactile Array (DTA)

Real-time contact sensing

Detects pressure, slip, force changes, and contact location at the fingertip.

Closed-loop adaptation

Adjusts grip, pose, and force when objects shift, deform, or become occluded.

Last-millimeter control

Loops-in tactile feedback in real time for fine control on contact-rich manipulation tasks.

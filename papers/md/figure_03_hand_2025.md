## Description

The Figure 03 humanoid robot marks the point where Figure AI moves from concept to large-scale reality. This third-generation humanoid is designed for safe, everyday collaboration with people, showing a softer, knit-clad exterior that feels closer to human environments than factory cages.

At its core, Figure 03 runs on Helix, Figure’s in-house vision-language-action model that connects perception, reasoning, and control into one generalist brain — giving the robot true autonomy beyond scripted demos.

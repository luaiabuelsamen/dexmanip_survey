## What the numbers say

**The sections do not share a rhythm.** They run from 713 to 6044 words, a factor of eight. A
reader cannot form an expectation of how long a section will be, which is part of what makes a
document feel disorganised even when each part is sound.

**The introduction has no internal structure.** Two thousand words in one undivided run is the
hardest part of the paper to navigate, and it is the part every reader reads.

**Section 5 is the only section that goes three levels deep.** Nine subsections and eight
sub-subsections against a paper that is otherwise one level. Either the rest is under-structured
or this one is over-structured, and the inconsistency is visible as you flip through.

**The evaluation section is the longest in the paper**, longer than the training section it is
supposed to serve. Some of that is justified, because the protocol is the survey's own
contribution, but 6044 words is longer than most surveys give to any single section.

**The conclusion is a tenth the length of the section before it.** After 6000 words of protocol
the paper stops in 713.

## What this does not tell us

Nothing here says the content is wrong. Every one of these sections survived a review against its
sources. The complaint is about shape, and shape is fixable without touching a claim.
